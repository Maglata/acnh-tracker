Privacy policy

ACNH Critter Tracker is a free, fan-made tool for tracking which Animal Crossing: New Horizons critters you have caught and donated. It has no server of its own and collects no analytics.

What is stored

Only your tracker progress: which critters are marked as caught or donated, and your hemisphere setting.

Where it is stored

  • In your browser (localStorage), always.
  • In your own Google Drive, only if you choose “Sign in with Google”. The progress is saved as one file, acnh-progress.json, in the app's hidden data folder (Google Drive “appDataFolder”).

Google user data

The app requests only the drive.appdata permission. This lets it read and write its own data file. It cannot see, open or change any other files in your Google Drive. It does not read your name, email address, contacts or any other Google data.

Communication with Google happens directly between your browser and Google. Your data and your sign-in token are never sent to the app's author or to any third party. The temporary sign-in token is kept in your browser session only. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Browser data: use “Reset” in the app or clear the site data in your browser.
  • Google Drive data: open Google Drive settings, then Manage apps, then ACNH Tracker, then Delete hidden app data. You can remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
